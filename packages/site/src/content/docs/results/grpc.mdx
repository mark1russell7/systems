These are the results of the gRPC cells, from 2026-10-19. The client is grpc-js 1.14.6 on Node 24.21.0, with one channel at the defaults of grpc-js. Each operation is a unary call of `grpc.health.v1.Health/Check`.

- **The servers.** The server of the harness is a server of grpc-go v1.84.0, built from its tag with Go 1.27.2, at the defaults of grpc-go. It can hold a reply for a cell. etcd v3.6.15 with one member serves the health service too.
- **T01** is the direct pair. The client and the server are in their own network namespaces, with a pair of veth devices between them. A border of O03 has a router between them.

The machine documents give each band: `grpc-js`, `grpc-go-server` and `etcd-server`. Each fact comes from the source at its tag. The algebra composes the documents over Linux TCP.

CI started the cells at main in the run [39288251967](https://github.com/mark1russell7/systems/actions/runs/39288251967), in 20 shards on the hosted runners of GitHub. The kernel is Linux 6.17.0-1022-azure, with HZ 1000. Each cell has 11 runs in 2 fresh environments, with one warm-up run in each environment. A `fin` stops the server, thus each of its runs has its own environment.

Each of the 32 cells holds or is a measurement. 31 cells hold, and the race of two deadlines is a measurement. The cells counted 352 runs, and no run was void.

## What the cells found

- **grpc-js has no detector at its defaults.** Its keepalive is off, a call has no deadline, and the connect has no timeout. It sets no TCP keepalive. Thus a black hole and a frozen server end nothing in 60 s, against each server.
- **The server finds a client that is gone.** grpc-go gives each accepted connection a TCP_USER_TIMEOUT of 20 s, because its keepalive is on. With the TCP keepalive of Go (15 s idle, 15 s between probes, 9 probes), the kernel of the server aborts an idle connection to a gone client 30.599 s after the last segment.
- **etcd gets no TCP_USER_TIMEOUT.** etcd serves gRPC and HTTP on one port through cmux, and grpc-go sets TCP_USER_TIMEOUT only on a TCPConn of Go. The listener of etcd sets the keepalive idle to 30 s. Thus the same abort comes 165 s after the last segment. Behind a router that forgot the flow, the kernel of etcd aborted the socket at 168.689 s.
- **Two deadlines race.** A call with a deadline gets a timer of the same time at the server of grpc-go, from the header `grpc-timeout`. The server resets the stream at its end. The deadline of grpc-js gives DEADLINE_EXCEEDED, and the reset of the server gives CANCELLED. With a deadline of 4 s, 2 of 11 calls got CANCELLED and 9 got DEADLINE_EXCEEDED, at a median of 4.004 s. Thus that cell is a measurement.
- **The policy for PINGs.** A PING less than MinTime after the one before is a strike, and the third strike gives GOAWAY `too_many_pings`. The first PING clears the strikes, because the server wrote a reply since the last one. Thus with a keepalive of 4 s against a MinTime of 5 s, the GOAWAY comes at the fourth PING. The call ended 16.041 s after the start of the client to grpc-go, and 16.029 s to etcd.
- **A reset, a stop and a stall.** A reset of a call gives ECONNRESET in 1.1 ms to grpc-go and in 1.4 ms to etcd. The FIN of a stopped server ends an idle connection in 43 µs. A reset of the SYN fails the first call with UNAVAILABLE in 16 ms to 17 ms. An unreachable host with `tcp_retries2` = 3 gives EHOSTUNREACH 3.120 s after the first retransmission. After a stall of 1 s, the reply comes back at 1.681 s from grpc-go and at 1.675 s from etcd.

## The cells that wait

The plan of R3 has more cells over these systems. The catalogs give a reason for each cell that waits:

- A restart of the server at its own address, and a fault timed to a reply, need features that the harness does not have.
- A block of the event loop of Node waits for a document of how late the timers of Node are after it.
- grpc-js sets TCP_NODELAY on each connection, thus a write-write-read does not wait for Nagle.
- etcd holds no reply of a call by itself. Caddy 2.11 serves no gRPC at its defaults: that cell is a cell of T02.
- The array stage gives each cell at the defaults a practice profile. Two profiles set knobs of grpc-js, and their three cells start: DP24 (a keepalive of 60 s or 300 s) and DP44 (a deadline of 4 s). The other profiles set no knob that a cell reads, thus 14 array cells are aliases of their cells at the defaults. 10 array cells wait for the reason of their cell.

## Where the data comes from

Each runner wrote a raw record of each of its cells. A record has the cell and the manifests of its environments. It also has each warm-up run, and each run. A run has its kernel records, the lines of the client and the lines of the server of the harness.

The script `packages/site/scripts/grpc.ts` reads each record with the code of the harness. It stops when a verdict disagrees with the summary of the run. It also stops when a prediction disagrees with the catalog at HZ 1000. It writes the copy that this page shows, and that copy names no machine. The cells and their predictions are in the [catalogs](https://github.com/mark1russell7/systems/tree/main/packages/cells).
