The cells operate on the runners of GitHub. Some runs do not give a clean result. This page gives one ledger for each workflow of cells, with one entry for each event.

An event is one of these:

- **A void run.** The run did not count. For example, the VM was not quiet in 300 s, or the cell was not at rest.
- **A hung shard.** The shard was cancelled or hit its timeout, and its step waited for a quiet VM for half of its time or more.
- **A cancelled shard.** A person or GitHub cancelled the shard.
- **A step timeout.** The step hit its bound, `timeout-minutes`.
- **A failed job.** The job failed for a cause outside the cells, for example a test of the snapshots. A cell that fails its prediction is a result, not an event.

Each entry gives the run, the shard, the cell, the step, the duration, the cause and a link to the job. A failed test is a fault of the code under test, thus it is an entry but not a flake. The flake rate of a workflow is the share of its shards with one flake or more. The flake rate of a dataplane is the share of its runs with an event.

## How the entries come

No entry is written by hand. The script `scripts/ci.ts` of the site reads a run with `gh`. It reads the jobs, the artifacts, and the annotations and the log of each job that did not succeed. Then it adds the entries to `src/data/ci/<workflow>.json`.

```sh
pnpm exec tsx packages/site/scripts/ci.ts <run id>...
```

A second scan of a run adds no entry twice. The entry of a job stays after its log expires.

## The idle share for Cilium

Before each run, the lab waits for a quiet VM: 60% of the CPUs idle, and no lab container of another agent. Since SYS-43, the wait stops after 300 s, and the run is void with its reason.

The share of 60% holds for a dataplane while 5% of its runs or fewer are void by this wait. A hung shard with this cause counts as one such run. The column "The share of 60% idle" of each table applies this rule.
